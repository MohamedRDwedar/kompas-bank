Privacy

Kompas, a personal budgeting app for one household. Last updated 4 October 2026.

Who this is

Kompas is a private, non-commercial app built and used by its owner for their own household finances. It is not offered to the public.

What the app does with bank data

When you connect a bank through Enable Banking (PSD2), the app on your phone fetches your accounts, balances and transactions with the consent you give at your bank. That consent lasts up to 180 days and you can withdraw it at your bank or in the app at any time.

All data is stored on your phone only, in the app's local database. There is no Kompas server: nothing is uploaded to, stored by or shared with the developer or any third party for advertising, profiling or sale.

What leaves the phone

  • Requests to Enable Banking and, through it, to your bank, to read the accounts you consented to.
  • If you switch on automatic sorting, shop names, item names and cleaned descriptions and amounts go to the AI service you chose. Account numbers (IBANs) are never sent.
  • Backups go only to a folder you pick, encrypted with your passphrase.

Keys and security

The Enable Banking key and session are stored encrypted in the phone's secure storage. They are never logged and never leave the phone.

Your choices

You can disconnect a bank, delete its data, or erase everything from the app's settings. Removing the app removes all its data.

Contact

For data protection questions, use the email address given to Enable Banking for this application.
