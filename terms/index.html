Use

Kompas is a private, non-commercial app used by its owner to see their own household's bank transactions, receipts and budget. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and it gives no financial, tax or legal advice.

Bank access

Bank data is read through Enable Banking with the account holder's own consent, read-only. Kompas never initiates payments.
